Request deletion by email

The operator currently accepts requests by email and processes them manually after verifying your identity. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app or signing in again.

Email shintaaa.apps@gmail.com to request deletion. Use the subject “ClipMark account deletion request” and state that you want your ClipMark account and its associated data deleted.

Do not send passwords, Google verification codes or one-time passwords, authentication tokens, or identity documents. The operator will not perform deletion based only on the sender address. The operator will contact you to verify the target account and your intention to delete it. Receiving a request does not mean deletion is complete. If verification is not possible, the operator will not delete the data and will let you know.

Open these instructions from the app

Open “Cloud sync” and choose “Open deletion request instructions”, or open “Privacy and terms” and choose “Account deletion”. Opening these instructions does not send a request or delete data.

Data covered by deletion

Deletion covers the verified ClipMark Firebase Authentication account and its associated data in Firestore managed by the operator, including memos, URLs, tags, ordering, playback information and sync information. Other Google services, YouTube's own data and other ClipMark accounts are not included.

Device data, backups and retention exceptions

Manual server processing cannot remotely erase data on your device or backups managed by the operating system or device manufacturer. The operator will separately explain how to remove device data and prevent old data from syncing again. The operator does not claim that historical analytics, operational logs and third-party backups are all erased at the same time.

If the operator needs to retain minimal records for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security or handling your request, the operator will explain the data, reason and retention period individually. The policy is not to retain all data indefinitely without a reason. The completion notice will distinguish what was deleted from what remains.

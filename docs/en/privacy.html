SHN_apps (the “Operator”) handles information about users of ClipMark (the “App”) as follows.

1. Information collected and stored

The information below includes analytics collected by earlier versions. The initial release stops collecting new Analytics data. This does not mean past analytics data has been deleted.

  • User ID, display name, email address and profile image URL provided through Google sign-in
  • Video URLs, video timestamps, memos, tags, and dates added or updated that you save
  • How often videos are opened from ClipMark and the date and time last played
  • Video IDs, titles, channel information, thumbnails, video length and public comments obtained through the YouTube Data API
  • Technical information needed to provide the service, such as sync status and data update times
  • App instance ID, device and app information, approximate region, and usage information such as app launches and feature use

Public comments are processed only while generating clip suggestions. Full comment text is not stored permanently in the App's database.

Usage analytics do not send memo text, search terms, video or stream URLs, video IDs, titles, channel names or tag names.

2. Purposes of use

  • Saving, searching, editing, playing and syncing ClipMarks across devices
  • Displaying video and channel information and clip suggestions derived from comments
  • Troubleshooting, preventing misuse and improving the service
  • Aggregating and analyzing usage and improving features and usability

3. External services

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Analytics for Firebase), Google Sign-In and YouTube Data API Services. Each provider's terms and policies apply to how information is handled by those services.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Firebase privacy and security

4. YouTube API data

The App uses YouTube API Services. API data such as video titles, channel names and thumbnails is managed under a policy of refreshing or deleting it within 30 days of retrieval. Memos and tags you create are not YouTube API data and are kept until the account is deleted.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Except where required by law, user information is not sold or provided to advertising businesses or other third parties. Data is sent to Google services to the extent needed to provide the service.

6. Retention and deletion

Requests to delete an account and associated data are accepted through the existing email contact in the deletion instructions. The Operator processes them manually after verifying identity. You can request deletion even if you cannot use the App. Receipt of a request and completion of deletion are separate.

The scope covers the Firebase account and associated Firestore data managed by the Operator. Data on devices, operating-system or device-manufacturer backups, past analytics and operational logs are checked separately; the Operator explains the scope completed and what remains. If minimal records must be kept for legal obligations, security or handling the request, the Operator explains the data, reason and period.

7. Security

Access controls allow only the authenticated user to read and write that user's own Firestore data, and reasonable security measures are taken.

8. Your choices

The initial release stops collecting new Analytics data and provides no way to enable it. Data is not collected even if an earlier consent setting remains. This does not mean past analytics data has been erased. If collection resumes in the future, an explanation and opportunity to choose will be provided again. You can review and revoke Google connections in your Google account settings.

9. Changes

This policy may change as features, laws or terms change. Important changes will be announced on this page or in the App.
